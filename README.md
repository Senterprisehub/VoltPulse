# VoltPulse

> Stop guessing how fast your phone is charging — see the real numbers in real time.

VoltPulse is a precision battery health, charging wattage, and power protection utility designed for modern Android devices. Whether you want to verify if your charger is delivering true Super Fast Charging or check how your battery is behaving over time, VoltPulse gives you the exact information you need.

## ⚡ Real-Time Charging Wattage & Speed Detection

Know immediately if a faulty cable or weak adapter is slowing you down.

- Live hardware telemetry for power in Watts (W), electrical current (mA), voltage (mV), and battery temperature (°C)
- 4-tier speed classification for Slow Charging, Standard Charging, Fast Charging, and Super Fast Charging
- Accurate system charge-time sync aligned with your device's hardware battery controller
- Real-time estimates for time remaining to 80% and 100% full charge

## 🔊 Customizable Smart Voice Alerts

Never leave your phone cooking on the charger again. VoltPulse speaks clear, hands-free voice notifications with independent on/off switches for every milestone.

- 100% Full Charge Voice Alert: Announces when charging has completed
- 80% Battery Health Protector: Notifies you at the optimal 80% mark to reduce high-voltage stress
- Smart Low-Battery Milestones: Alerts only at critical 20%, 10%, and 5% levels
- Independent controls for voice, sound, and vibration for each alert type

## 🛡️ Overcharge Guard & Smart Plug Automation

Protect your battery while charging overnight or at your desk.

- Full-screen overcharge guard with a high-visibility wake screen when your device hits 100%
- Smart plug webhook support for Home Assistant, IFTTT, Tasmota, and custom HTTP webhooks
- Hardware cut-off support for rooted devices

## 🔌 Charger & Cable Benchmark Tester

Not all USB-C cables are created equal. Run a dedicated cable and charger benchmark to measure peak wattage, average current, voltage stability, and thermal rise, then save results to compare your accessories over time.

## 📊 Session History & Lock Screen Live Monitor

- Live notification bar showing charging wattage, battery temperature, and time-to-full
- Historical session logs for charging and discharging activity
- Private, offline-first local storage for diagnostics, benchmarks, and settings

## Why VoltPulse?

VoltPulse helps you:

- Verify your charger and cable are actually delivering the expected power
- Protect your battery from overcharging and heat stress
- Track charging performance over time with detailed session history
- Stay informed with smart alerts and live monitoring

Download VoltPulse today to test your chargers, guard against overcharging, and extend your battery's lifespan.
